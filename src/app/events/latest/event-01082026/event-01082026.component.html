Farewell Programme for Ex-Principal Chief Executive Officer and Smt. Kabita Rai

1st August, 2026

A farewell programme was organized in honour of our Ex-Principal Chief Executive Officer and Smt. Kabita Rai, Ex-Jr. Instructor. The event was held on 1st August 2026 at Bustee Resort, Ranka.

During the ceremony, the retiring staff were warmly felicitated with traditional khadas, mementos, and gifts in heartfelt appreciation of their dedicated service, leadership, and enduring contributions to the Sikkim Khadi & Village Industries Board.
