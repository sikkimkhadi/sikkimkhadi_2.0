Visit of Honourable Union Minister of Culture & Tourism to Dodak Khadi Woollen Centre

29th May, 2026

On 29th May 2026, Shri Gajendra Singh Shekhawat Ji, Honourable Union Minister of Culture & Tourism, Government of India, visited the Dodak Khadi Woollen Centre in Soreng District. The visit marked a significant milestone for the local weaving community, highlighting the center's heritage of woollen craft and traditional Sikkim khadi production.

The delegation from the Sikkim Khadi & Village Industries Board (SKVIB) was led by Chief Executive Officer Shri Dorjee Sherpa, alongside Assistant Executive Officer Shri Chewang Topden Bhutia and Office Superintendent Shri Chewang Namgyal Bhutia. The Board represented the organization and extended a warm traditional welcome to the Honourable Union Minister, introducing him to the active projects and artisan initiatives at the Dodak center.

The distinguished gathering of guests included:

  • Honourable MLAs:
    • Shri Madhan Cintury (Zoom Salghari Constituency)
    • Shri Adity Golay (Soreng Chakung Constituency)
    • Shri Erung Tshering Lepcha (Rinchenpong Constituency)
  • Government Dignitaries:
    • Mrs. Hondala Gyaltsen (Secretary Tourism)
    • Shri Dhiraj Subedi (District Collector Soreng)
  • Honourable Chairpersons, Local Area Panchayat, and members of the public.

During the visit, the Union Minister interacted with the local weavers and artisans, observing the craftsmanship and operations at the woollen center. Discussions focused on promoting traditional khadi weaving, integrating regional tourism with local crafts, and strengthening support for rural livelihoods.
