Working for Sikkim's rural artisans since 1963

Sikkim Khadi & Village Industries Board (SKVIB) is an autonomous body of the Government of Sikkim under the Commerce & Industries Department. It was established in 1963, and its functions are set out in the Sikkim Khadi & Village Industries Board Act, 1996.

What the Board does

  • Creates employment for rural artisans in khadi and village industries
  • Produces khadi and village industry goods for sale
  • Builds self-reliance and a strong rural community spirit
  • Runs government schemes for rural employment
  • Trains and supports new khadi institutions, societies and village industry units
